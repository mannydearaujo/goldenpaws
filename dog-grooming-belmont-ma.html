Grooming a short drive from the Town of Homes

From Belmont Center, Cushing Square, or Waverley, Lexington is a straightforward run over Route 60 and across town. Belmont is a settled, family-heavy town with a lot of well-loved dogs, and Belmont owners tend to value the same thing we do: steady, predictable grooming where the coat is genuinely cared for and the dog is comfortable throughout.

Habitat trails and weekend coats

Between the Mass Audubon Habitat sanctuary, Rock Meadow, and Beaver Brook, Belmont dogs get plenty of outdoor time, and the coat collects burrs, dirt, and loose fur to match. A full bath and high-velocity dry resets things, and for double-coated shedders our de-shedding treatment makes a visible difference at home. A bath and brush-out keeps lighter coats tidy between full cuts.

What we offer

Full grooming with a breed-appropriate cut, bath and brush-out, de-shedding, a standalone nail trim, and a gentle first groom for first-timers. A full groom is a bath with professional shampoo and conditioner, a high-velocity dry, a breed-appropriate haircut, ear cleaning, nail trim, anal gland expression, and a cologne or bandana finish, shaped to your dog.

Breeds we groom from Belmont
