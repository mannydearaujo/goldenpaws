City dogs, small spaces, and a lot of shedding

In a triple-decker or a condo, shedding is not a minor annoyance. It is on the couch, the rug, and your clothes by Wednesday. Our de-shedding treatment is the most practical thing a Medford apartment dog can get: a high-velocity blow-out with a de-shedding shampoo and conditioner that strips the loose undercoat at the salon instead of leaving it for your vacuum. For double-coated breeds it makes weeks of difference at home. For dogs who do not need a full cut, a regular bath and brush-out keeps things under control too.

Fells dogs and what the trails leave behind

Plenty of Medford dogs get their exercise in the Middlesex Fells, the Sheepfold field, the trails off the Fellsway, and they come home with the evidence: dirt, debris, the odd tangle, and a coat full of whatever they rolled in. A proper bath and dry resets all of that, and on longer or curlier coats it is the moment to catch mats early, before they tighten up against the skin. We would rather brush a coat out than have to shave it down, and getting dogs in on a steady schedule is how we keep it that way.
