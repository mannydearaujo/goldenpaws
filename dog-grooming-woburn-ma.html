Frequently asked questions

How long is the drive from Woburn?

My dog sheds everywhere after Horn Pond. Can you help?

Yes, that is what the de-shedding treatment is for. A high-velocity blow-out with de-shedding shampoo and conditioner removes the loose undercoat that is ending up on your floors and furniture, and it noticeably cuts shedding for weeks afterward.
