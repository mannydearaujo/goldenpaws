Trail dogs, burrs, and what Bedford coats walk in with

Bedford is a walking town. Between the Minuteman Bikeway running up to the old Bedford Depot, the Great Meadows refuge, and all the conservation land threaded through the neighborhoods, the dogs we see from this side of the line tend to be outdoor dogs, and their coats show it. We get the spaniel who found every puddle, the doodle carrying half a field of burrs, the retriever blowing coat in June. A real bath and a proper high-velocity dry do more than freshen things up. That is when loose undercoat, mats behind the ears, and whatever the trail left behind actually get dealt with. For the heavy shedders, our de-shedding treatment is built for exactly this.

What we do

Full grooming with a breed-appropriate cut, bath and brush-out, de-shedding for double coats, a standalone nail trim when that is all you need, and a slow, gentle first groom for puppies who have never been on a table before. A full groom covers a bath with professional shampoo and conditioner, a high-velocity dry, a breed-appropriate haircut, ear cleaning, nail trim, anal gland expression, and a cologne or bandana finish. None of it is assembly-line. We work to your dog's coat and temperament, not a stopwatch.

Frequently asked questions

How close is Golden Paws to Bedford, really?

Closer than most Bedford owners expect. We are at 395 Lowell St in Lexington, directly over the shared town border, about five to ten minutes from Bedford Center down Route 4/225. It is one of the shortest drives to a professional groomer you will find from Bedford.

My dog comes home from the trails muddy and matted. Can you handle that?

That is a normal Tuesday for us. Trail dogs come in with mud, burrs, and tangles, and a full groom is exactly where that gets sorted: bathed out, dried properly, then brushed or de-matted depending on how far gone the coat is. If matting is severe, we will always talk through the kindest option for the dog before we start.

What's included in a full groom?

A bath with professional shampoo and conditioner, a high-velocity blow-dry, a breed-appropriate haircut, ear cleaning, nail trim, anal gland expression, and a cologne or bandana finish, all tailored to your dog's coat and breed.

Can I book just a nail trim or a bath without the haircut?

Yes. A standalone nail trim and a bath-and-brush without a full cut are both available, handy between full grooms, or for dogs who do not need a haircut at all. Just say so when you call.
