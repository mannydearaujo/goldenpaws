What is the difference between a bath and brush-out and a full groom?

The bath and brush-out covers everything in the grooming process except the haircut. A full groom includes all of the above steps plus a breed-appropriate scissor or clipper cut. For dogs who do not need a haircut at every visit — such as Labs, Goldens, Beagles, and other shorter-coated breeds — the bath and brush-out is the right service. For Doodles, Poodles, and other continuously growing coat breeds, the bath and brush-out is a practical option between full groom appointments to keep the coat clean and tangle-free without requiring the full time and cost of a haircut visit every time.

Why does professional blow-drying matter?

The blow-dry step is one that significantly differentiates professional grooming from a home bath. A professional high-velocity dryer removes moisture from the coat completely and rapidly — preventing the skin problems that can develop when a dense or double coat stays damp close to the skin for hours after a home wash. It also physically blows loose shed hair out of the coat rather than leaving it trapped in the fur, which is particularly valuable for heavy-shedding breeds. For Doodles and other curly-coated breeds, proper blow-dry technique also prevents the tight, matted curls that form when a wet coat is left to air-dry. Professional blow-drying is not a luxury step — it is a core part of responsible coat care.

How often should my dog receive a bath and brush-out?

Most dogs benefit from a professional bath and brush-out every 4 to 8 weeks. Active dogs, dogs that swim or spend time outdoors, and heavy-shedding breeds often benefit from bathing closer to every 4 weeks. Dogs with sensitive skin or dry coats may do better at 6 to 8 weeks. Our groomers will assess your dog's coat and skin condition and recommend the frequency that makes the most sense for your individual dog.
