How often should a Concord dog be professionally groomed?

For most breeds, a grooming interval of every 6 to 8 weeks maintains coat health effectively. Dogs with continuously growing or mat-prone coats — Doodles, Poodles, Spaniels, Shih Tzus — benefit from every 4 to 6 weeks. Our groomers will assess your dog's coat and make a specific recommendation.
